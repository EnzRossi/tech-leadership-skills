# Sources and attribution

Reviewed 2026-09-16. Sources inform interpretation; none can establish the status of a particular project.

| Source | Contribution and limit |
|---|---|
| [DORA's software delivery performance metrics](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/), DORA | Current guidance describes five metrics and emphasizes application/service context. Delivery performance is an input, not a project-completion score. |
| [The SPACE of Developer Productivity](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-space-of-developer-productivity-theres-more-to-it-than-you-think/), Forsgren et al., ACM Queue, 2021 | Productivity is multidimensional; activity alone is insufficient. We do not implement a SPACE survey or benchmark here. |
| [The Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html), Schwaber and Sutherland, 2020 | Definition of Done and shared understanding of quality. Missing links in an export do not prove those practices are absent. |
| [Reliable Product Launches at Scale](https://sre.google/sre-book/reliable-product-launches/), Google SRE | Operational and launch readiness as explicit engineering work. Apply proportionately to the system's exposure. |

## EnzRossi contribution

The artifact-capability checks, evidence-to-commitment trace, five-area signal catalog, separate health/confidence decision rules, and proportionate intervention memo are the repository's synthesis. These are review heuristics, not an empirically calibrated project risk model. Corroboration is not causal proof, and the color definitions must be interpreted against the commitment under review.
