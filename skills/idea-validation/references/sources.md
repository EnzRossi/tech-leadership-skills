# Sources and attribution

Reviewed 2026-09-18. These sources inform the method; they do not validate its effectiveness or establish facts about a user's idea.

| Source | Contribution and limit |
|---|---|
| [The Four Big Risks](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/), Marty Cagan, 2017 | Value, usability, feasibility and business-viability risks assessed separately. A product-discovery framing, not a market forecast. |
| [The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com/), Rob Fitzpatrick, 2013 | Conversation rules: ask about the person's life and past behaviour, not the idea; treat compliments and hypotheticals as noise; look for commitments. Informs the demand-evidence hierarchy. |
| [Lean Startup principles](https://theleanstartup.com/principles), Eric Ries | Validated learning and build-measure-learn; an idea is a hypothesis to test with the smallest experiment. Does not prescribe which tests fit which uncertainty. |
| [Pretotyping](https://www.pretotyping.org/), Alberto Savoia | Test whether people want the product before building it, using your own data rather than opinions. Informs the fake-door, pre-order and concierge tests. |
| [How to Get Startup Ideas](https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html), Paul Graham, 2012 | Prefer a small group with an intense need over a large group with mild interest; organic ideas from the founder's own problems; the "schlep" filter. Essay on founders' experience, not a validation procedure. |
| [How to Evaluate Startup Ideas](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-week-8-recap-adora-cheung-on-prioritizing-your-time-and-kevin-hale-on-evaluating-startup-ideas/), Kevin Hale, Y Combinator Startup School, 2019 | An idea as problem, solution and insight; start with the problem, not the technology; the founder's unfair advantage. Oriented to venture-scale growth, which not every idea seeks. |
| [Value Proposition Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas), Alexander Osterwalder and Strategyzer | Mapping customer jobs, pains and gains to an offer and testing fit against evidence. A design tool; fit on paper is not demand. |
| [Disciplined Entrepreneurship](https://www.d-eship.com/), Bill Aulet, MIT | Beachhead market, end-user and decision-making-unit mapping, and the customer acquisition process as explicit steps. Book content; the site is a hub, not the full method. |
| [7 Powers](https://7powers.com/), Hamilton Helmer, 2016 | Sources of persistent advantage such as scale, network economies, counter-positioning, switching costs, branding, cornered resources and process power. Written for established strategy; applied here only to ask what prevents copying. |
| [Why Start-ups Fail](https://hbr.org/2021/05/why-start-ups-fail), Tom Eisenmann, Harvard Business Review, 2021 | Failure patterns including false starts without customer research and bad bedfellows. Research on funded startups; not a base rate for any specific idea. |

AI-fit checks reuse the problem-first and evaluation reasoning attributed in the [AI initiative evaluator sources](../../ai-initiative-evaluator/references/sources.md).

## EnzRossi contribution

The internal/external split, the geography-first market reading, the buyer-and-route map with its demand-evidence hierarchy, the AI "who else can ship this" check, the five-verdict scale with matched cheapest tests, and the two-sided honesty rule are this repository's synthesis. Customer discovery, lean experiments and risk separation are established practice; EnzRossi does not claim to have invented them. A memo produced with this workflow is an initial assessment for a human decision, not market research.

## September 2026 revision: evidence and experiment design

Primary pages reviewed 2026-09-18. The rules below are adaptations, not empirically established success thresholds.

| Source | Contribution and limit |
|---|---|
| [Start with the most critical hypotheses](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/how-to-test-your-idea-start-with-the-most-critical-hypotheses), Strategyzer | Prioritize consequential assumptions with weak evidence. Does not calibrate numerical success probabilities. |
| [Validate your ideas with the Test Card](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/validate-your-ideas-with-the-test-card), Strategyzer | Predefine hypothesis, test, measurement and threshold. Cohort, cap and explicit inconclusive actions are additions in this repository. |
| [Testing Your Business Model: A Reference Guide](https://strategyzr.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/resources/testing-your-business-model-a-reference-guide.pdf), Strategyzer, p. 7 | Distinguishes nonbinding letters of intent from presales. Buyer authority, refundability and follow-through checks are our synthesis; this is not advice about enforceability. Do not adopt the guide's outdated suggestion to collect and destroy card details. |
| [Using in-depth interviews](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-in-depth-interviews), GOV.UK | Recruit actual/likely users; explore concrete experiences with open, non-leading questions. Qualitative evidence does not estimate market conversion. |
| [Critical event and product usage interval](https://amplitude.com/books/mastering-retention/your-critical-event-and-product-usage-interval), Amplitude | Measure retention around meaningful activity and natural usage frequency. Product-analytics heuristics are not universal idea-validation gates. |
| [Using performance data to improve your service](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/using-data-to-improve-your-service-an-introduction), GOV.UK | Outcome, completion and cost measurement informs internal-tool validation; adapting public-service metrics to internal workflows is our synthesis. |
| [Experimentation best practices](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/playfab/live-service-management/game-configuration/experiments/experimentation-keys), Microsoft Learn | Plan duration/sample and protect test validity. A/B guidance does not make small interviews or pilots statistically conclusive. |

The claim-specific evidence checks, explicit inconclusive outcome, contextual proceed/stop rules and experiment card extend the original synthesis. They require testing against real decisions; published frameworks alone do not demonstrate skill quality.
