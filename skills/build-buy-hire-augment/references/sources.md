# Sources and attribution

Reviewed 2026-09-16. EnzRossi sells engineering services; these sources and the option comparison do not endorse EnzRossi or any supplier.

| Source | Contribution and limit |
|---|---|
| [The Sourcing Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-sourcing-and-consultancy-playbooks/the-sourcing-playbook-html), UK Cabinet Office | Proportional delivery-model assessment, whole-life costs, testing and transition planning. Public procurement requirements are not imported as requirements on private companies. |
| [Utility vs Strategic Dichotomy](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/UtilityVsStrategicDichotomy.html), Ross Pettit, 2010 | Treat strategic and utility capabilities differently. Differentiation is one consideration, not a universal build/buy rule. |
| [Don't get locked up into avoiding lock-in](https://martinfowler.com/articles/oss-lockin.html), Gregor Hohpe, 2019 | Evaluate switching costs against value; internally built systems can create lock-in too. |
| [Managing technical lock-in in the cloud](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/managing-technical-lock-in-in-the-cloud), UK Government Digital Service | Plan portability and replacement; accepting some lock-in can be reasonable. |
| [A review of the IT outsourcing literature: insights for practice](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/26386/), Lacity, Khan and Willcocks, 2009 | Client/supplier capabilities and relationship management matter. Historical research does not set current hiring speeds, rates or outsourcing success probabilities. |

## EnzRossi contribution

Separating solution choice from delivery/ownership, screening verified constraints, symmetric comparison of internal and external routes, and requiring evidence of receiving-team capability are this repository's synthesis. It claims no novel economic theory, validated score, or optimal sourcing formula. Ownership and access arrangements must be established for the actual engagement.
