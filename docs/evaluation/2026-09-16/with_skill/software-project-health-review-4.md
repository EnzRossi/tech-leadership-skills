# Project health decision: warehouse management system

Executive team · Evidence as of the supplied account; report date and release target unspecified

## Recommendation

**Health: Not assessable against the final release commitment · Confidence: Medium in the reported local delivery threats, Low in the release forecast.** Tell executives that completed phases do not establish launch readiness. Phase 4 started three weeks late, phase 5 lacks an ERP owner, and QA leadership is vacant. Resolve ERP allocation and quality ownership now. A final target and remaining acceptance plan are needed before claiming the project is on track or forecasting a delay. The 15% underspend does not establish savings.

Phase 4 supplies a concrete example; phase 5 exposes continued risk, but does not yet prove the final date impossible. The quality concern is whether acceptance and release work has an accountable owner and sufficient capacity. More contractors will not resolve an ERP priority decision by themselves.
