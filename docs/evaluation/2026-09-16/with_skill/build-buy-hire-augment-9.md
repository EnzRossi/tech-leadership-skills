# Sourcing decision: Monthly analytics export

Founder · Evidence: supplied workload and requirements

## Recommendation

**Confidence: High.**

Keep the existing export and monthly reporting process. Do not commission a custom analytics platform or agency engagement. You report that the export already meets every requirement, takes 20 minutes once a month and has no expected growth. There is no demonstrated capability gap worth sourcing.

## Why this choice

The solution is to retain the existing capability, delivered by its current operator with a named business owner for the report. At the supplied workload, annual manual effort is 20 minutes × 12 reports = 240 minutes, or four hours. That bounds the reported effort available to recover; it is not a cash saving estimate and does not establish a business case for automation.

The strongest competing option would be a small improvement to the existing export if an actual reliability or access issue emerges. A bought analytics product could also meet a future expanded need, but no such requirement exists here. A custom platform or agency adds discovery, configuration or development, acceptance and ongoing ownership to solve a problem the current process already handles.

The founder's preference may reflect an unstated strategic objective. It should be evaluated as a new requirement with evidence, rather than treated as proof that the monthly report is inadequate. No external route is categorically disallowed; it simply lacks a justified outcome in this case.

## Cost, transition and exit

Use the same report outcome and one-year workload when comparing future proposals. Retention costs four reported staff-hours annually plus any actual existing tool cost. Build or buy would add initial effort, maintenance, migration, licenses where relevant, and eventual replacement. Prices are unknown, but no positive business requirement is supplied to justify those additions.

There is no transition to fund. Keep the report's location, export instructions and access understandable to a backup operator if that is not already true. This is routine continuity, not a new platform project. Do not create an agency “discovery” engagement solely to manufacture scope.

## Next action

Proposed owner: the report's business owner. Confirm the current export still meets requirements and retain it. Close the sourcing proposal unless the founder can identify a distinct valuable outcome that the current process cannot provide.

## What could change the recommendation

Revisit if requirements expand, workload materially grows, the export becomes unreliable or the consequences of error change. Each would need observed evidence. With the current complete fit and four-hour annual workload, waiting is an active, justified choice.
