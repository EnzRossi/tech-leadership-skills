# Project health decision: mobile app rewrite

Sponsor · Evidence as of the supplied account; report date and release year not supplied

## Recommendation

**Health: Not assessable · Confidence: Low for November 1 delivery.** Do not endorse the agency's green status yet. The 78% claim is not tied to accepted release scope, and the limited demos cannot establish whether the remaining app and release work are achievable by November 1. Equally, the evidence does not prove the project is failing. Obtain a demonstrated acceptance baseline and a dated remaining-work plan this week before deciding whether recovery intervention is needed.

The candidate threat is that essential app journeys, integrations or release activities remain unaccepted while the reported percentage creates confidence in November 1. The decisive check is to connect each required release outcome to working evidence and the remaining work needed for acceptance. Without durations and dependencies, none of these can yet be called the critical path. Capacity, operational readiness and the agreed launch definition remain unknown.

## What could change the recommendation

Three missing facts matter most: what November 1 actually promises; which core outcomes are already accepted; and whether the remaining dependency and release plan fits the time available. Obtain these through the baseline, demonstration and plan above. Credible accepted work and a feasible remaining path would support Green. An unmanaged integration threat would support Amber; a breached or unachievable commitment would support Red.
