# Idea validation: project-handoff app

External digital product · Small German design agencies

## Verdict

**Test before building — High confidence that this test is inconclusive; demand remains unknown.** Stop buying traffic now: the agreed €150 cap is exhausted. Do not kill the app on this result. The weakest point is that eligible owners never had a functioning booking route, and only four were verified against the planned 30. These are fixable test defects, not evidence of a fatal demand problem. Two booking clicks suggest initial interest, but establish neither bookings nor willingness to pay.

## Decisive evidence

Your hypothesis was that at least three of 30 eligible agency owners would book a paid trial. The 400 general-newsletter visits are not that denominator. Zero completed bookings through a form broken throughout the test cannot distinguish rejection from inability to book. Neither colleague’s proposed conclusion follows: you have neither proof of zero demand nor justification for unlimited traffic purchases. Problem severity, existing alternatives and commercial viability remain untested by these facts.

## One next step

Prepare a **separate, capped retest proposal** before committing more money. Proposed owner: whoever ran this test. Before launch, verify the booking flow end to end and specify a route to 30 verified eligible owners, a fixed observation window, and a newly agreed money and effort cap. Preserve the same offer and original rule: three or more completed paid-trial bookings passes to a bounded trial; fewer than three after 30 valid exposures fails and stops that offer. Insufficient eligible exposure or another broken flow is inconclusive: stop at the cap and review, without automatically extending. Record this run separately; clicks cannot carry forward as bookings.
