# Idea validation: UK gym rota-export add-on

External digital product · Independent UK gyms · AI dependency unspecified

## Verdict

**Test before building. Do not hire two engineers yet.** Confidence in this next step is high: owner willingness to pay £80/month is untested. This gap could be fixable; demand is neither validated nor disproven. Eight gyms trying an export is the strongest reason to investigate further.

## Hypothesis and decisive evidence

Provisional hypothesis: rota export creates enough recurring value for gym owners to pay £80/month instead of their current workflow. The problem’s cost, alternatives, why now and your advantage remain unknown.

The 12 instructor letters express conditional interest, subject to owner approval, without binding purchases. The 20 fully refundable £5 reservations show low-risk interest from meetup attendees; buyer authority and overlap with other groups are unknown. Neither establishes subscription demand.

Eight free, founder-assisted trials show initial use, not independent usability or paid value. None has reached another monthly rota: retention is **unobserved**, not zero. Sustainable delivery and acquisition economics are also unknown.

## Next test or bounded commitment

Proposed owner: founder. Reach the eight trial gyms’ authorized owners through their participating instructors. First document their current workflow, effort and errors; then offer a clearly scoped £80/month concierge pilot with explicit assistance and cancellation terms.

Track owners reached/offered, paying gyms/offered, and repeat paid exports/paying gyms whose next monthly rota is due. Record refusals, refunds, outcomes and founder delivery/acquisition time separately. Observe the next rota and subsequent renewal; review after each eligible gym’s second opportunity. Proposed cap: this cohort and existing manual delivery only, with a founder-hours ceiling agreed before starting.

Proposed rules: repeated paid value justifies a bounded follow-on pilot; consistent informed refusals or failed renewals warrant reshaping the offer. Unreached owners or cycles not yet due are inconclusive. No numerical hiring threshold is defensible yet.

## Questions that could change the decision

- What problem, current workaround and measurable cost do owners report?
- What delivery costs, founder-hours ceiling and hiring economics must this support?
