A supplies the most explicit numeric rule but never explains why three of eight should unlock a follow-on investment. B supplies detailed delivery/acquisition measurement but leaves ambiguous outcomes outside its decision branches. These are potentially material amplifications of usability/support evidence, though neither changes the warranted immediate decision.

For the handoff app, all three correctly stop spending, preserve three-of-30, reject zero-demand inference, and require a repaired, bounded eligible-cohort test. A makes a two-week window an explicit proposal and warns against pooling the broken run. C explains offer-level versus app-level conclusions. B reaches the same core decision in substantially fewer words, but omits the 400 visits required by the literal assertion. A and C miss the frozen word cap. No unsupported market or conversion claims were identified.

For the internal release, all three support the bounded release using actual adoption, observed outcomes, failed alternatives and ownership, while distinguishing capacity from cash savings and avoiding exact totals from medians. B provides the clearest task/error denominators and an inconclusive review outcome. A supplies explicit proposed rollback thresholds, although any error rise is rigid and median time below 20 could permit most of the observed gain to disappear. C is concise and provides quality/export rollback controls; its 60% median reduction is mathematically correct.

## Weak or incomplete assertions

- Numeric thresholds need contextual justification, but nonnumeric gates also need enough specificity to prevent post hoc success declarations. C passes the existing gym assertion while illustrating this gap.
- Separate inconclusive handling from threshold justification, and separate word limits from fabricated benchmarks/costs. Compound failures otherwise obscure the actual weakness.
- The supplied prompts do not state the frozen 300/700-word limits. Verify that these constraints were actually communicated to executors; unseen execution instructions cannot be assumed in this blind review.
- Mandatory recitation of 400 visits penalizes an otherwise correct denominator-based decision. Test whether general traffic is misused as eligible exposure unless full funnel reporting is itself a requested outcome.
- Add fidelity checks for claims about how much assistance was supplied or whether assistance was necessary.
- Monitoring-presence assertions do not distinguish proportionate, comparable regression criteria from over-sensitive or permissive proposed rules.
