# Idea validation: Project-handoff app

External · Digital · Small German design agencies · AI dependency unspecified

## Verdict

**Test before building — high confidence that this test is inconclusive; demand remains unknown.** Stop spending now because the agreed €150 cap is exhausted. Do not kill the app on this evidence. The weakest point is the test itself: only four verified eligible owners reached the page, and nobody could complete a booking. These are fixable test failures, not demonstrated absence of demand. Two booking clicks are the strongest encouraging signal, but they are not paid-trial bookings.

## What the evidence shows

Your hypothesis was that at least three of 30 eligible agency owners would book a paid trial of the handoff app. The 400 general-newsletter visits do not establish exposure to 30 eligible owners; only four visitors were verified eligible. The broken form also prevented the required behaviour throughout the test. Zero completed bookings therefore cannot distinguish rejection of the offer from inability to book. Nor can two clicks establish willingness to pay.

The original stopping rule concerned this offer, not every possible version of the app. Its demand threshold was not meaningfully tested. The spending limit, however, was reached and remains binding. Buying traffic until three bookings appear would replace a fixed test with an open-ended search for success.

## One next step

Write and agree a replacement test contract before any further spending: same offer, a booking path verified end to end, 30 verified eligible owners reached through direct recruitment, and a fixed deadline plus fresh cash and effort caps. Retain the original outcome rule: at least three completed paid-trial bookings passes; fewer than three after valid exposure stops that offer. Failure to reach the cohort or another booking outage is inconclusive, with work stopping at the cap. These replacement arrangements are proposed, not already authorised.
