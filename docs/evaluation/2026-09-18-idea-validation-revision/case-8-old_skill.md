# Idea validation: UK gym rota-export add-on

Founders · External · Digital · AI dependency unspecified · Independent UK gyms

## Verdict

**Test before building — high confidence that hiring is premature; low confidence about eventual demand.** Your weakest point is owner willingness to pay £80/month for recurring value. That is untested, not disproved, and potentially fixable. Eight gyms completed a real export, which supports further testing. Defer the two engineering hires and test paid use across actual monthly rota cycles with the existing assisted service.

## The idea as a hypothesis

Independent UK gym owners will pay £80/month because the add-on improves monthly rota exports enough to justify switching from their current process. This is a hypothesis: the current alternative, pain, time savings, urgency, why now and founder advantage are unspecified. Monthly scheduling supplies a possible recurring occasion, not proof of recurring value.

## What the evidence shows

- The 12 letters express instructors' conditional willingness to try. They are nonbinding and depend on owner approval; they do not establish purchasing authority or budget commitment. Owners are the next decision makers to reach, with budget authority still to confirm.
- The 20 fully refundable £5 reservations show some action, but little financial commitment. They do not validate an £80 monthly subscription. Whether reservers represent eligible gyms, control budgets or overlap with the other groups is unknown; do not add these counts together as customers.
- Eight free, founder-assisted exports support limited feasibility. They do not establish independent usability or paid value. Repeat use is **not yet observable**: no gym has reached its next monthly rota. This is neither retention nor churn evidence.
- Value remains unknown; usability is a concern because assistance was required; scalable feasibility and business viability are unknown. Acquisition effort, ongoing support and operating costs must be measured before judging margins. Competitors, substitutes, copyability and platform dependence are unassessed under the supplied-facts-only constraint. No market-size or cost estimate is justified.

## Cheapest test that could change the verdict

Run one founder-led paid concierge cohort with the eight trial gyms. Ask instructors for owner introductions, confirm who controls the budget, and offer the existing export service at £80/month. Track payment, completed exports, assistance time, direct costs and reasons for refusal. Cap delivery at these eight gyms, with no engineering hires, through each gym's next two monthly rota opportunities; record dates before starting.

**Proposed thresholds, to agree before running:** pass if at least three distinct owners pay £80 for the next cycle and those same gyms pay again and use it at the following cycle. Kill the current subscription proposition if none pays, or no initial payer renews and uses it when due. Intermediate results remain inconclusive and do not justify hiring. A pass supports a bounded next investment only after measured delivery costs and a credible acquisition route support it.

## Questions that still matter

What does each owner currently do, and what measurable loss does exporting cause? Can instructors secure owner meetings? Which export step required founder assistance? These answers determine whether the obstacle is access, value or delivery.
