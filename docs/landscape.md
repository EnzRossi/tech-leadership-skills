# Landscape and positioning

Independent review: 2026-09-16. This is a bounded sample, not a census or originality proof. Repository READMEs and selected workflows were inspected; advertised evaluations were not independently reproduced. Counts, star rankings and claims of inactivity are omitted because they do not establish usefulness.

## Overlap we should acknowledge

| Collection | Observed overlap | Implication for this repository |
|---|---|---|
| [bettersense](https://github.com/shwetank/bettersense) | AI PM problem/AI-fit and evaluation guidance; engineering-health and team-diagnosis workflows, including cited stakeholder evidence | Neither AI-fit screening nor evidence-based diagnosis is novel. Focus on commitment-level decisions and demonstrate incremental value with harder evidence cases. |
| [pm-skills](https://github.com/product-on-purpose/pm-skills) | Product workflows with examples, contracts and CI | Templates and validation are expected quality features, not differentiators by themselves. |
| [claude-skills](https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills) | Broad advisor and management coverage, including executive roles | A CTO persona or broad checklist adds little; constrain each workflow to one decision. |
| [ARCHON](https://github.com/Pouya-Mansournia/ARCHON) | CTO/principal-engineer decisions, domain references and complexity/trade-off checks | Technology leadership decision support already exists. Avoid claiming an empty category. |
| [design-org-skills](https://github.com/rastian/design-org-skills) | Leadership and organizational decision frameworks | Generic leadership guidance would dilute the technology-specific focus. |

The first research pass also listed manager, founder/operator and AI-prioritization collections. Their absence from this smaller table is not a finding that they are inactive or inferior. No competitor wording or template was imported.

## Positioning decision

Keep **EnzRossi Tech Leadership Skills** and the existing one-sentence positioning. They accurately describe the audience and scope. Rebranding would not solve the actual gaps: overconfident rules, bulky outputs and weakly evidenced evaluation claims.

The credible angle is narrow: AI investment gates with a usable evaluation contract; software delivery diagnosis tied to timestamped evidence; and sourcing that separates delivery from retained ownership and exposes the publisher's commercial incentive. These are combinations of established ideas, not exclusive methodologies.

Generic management advice, executive personas, universal scorecards and arbitrary benchmark numbers are easy to duplicate. A durable advantage would be a growing set of anonymized real decision failures, corrected methods, and retained comparisons showing where a strong model still needs the workflow. That evidence remains an adoption hypothesis until leaders use the outputs.

## Official guidance checked

- [Format specification](https://agentskills.io/specification): packaging/frontmatter and progressive disclosure. Compliance does not establish behavioral quality.
- [Authoring best practices](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/best-practices): focused instructions that add what a capable model does not reliably supply.
- [Evaluation guidance](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/evaluating-skills): paired comparisons, evidence for grades and inspection of outputs. This is guidance, not part of the normative packaging schema.
- [Description optimization](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/optimizing-descriptions): realistic positives and near misses, observed skill loading and held-out queries.
- [Anthropic skill-creator](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/skill-creator/SKILL.md): an implemented evaluation/iteration workflow, including baseline comparison and review artifacts.
- [OpenAI skill-creator](https://github.com/openai/skills/blob/main/skills/.system/skill-creator/SKILL.md): concise procedural knowledge, explicit reference-loading conditions and appropriate degrees of freedom. Optional client UI metadata is not a cross-client requirement.
- [Anthropic doc-coauthoring](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/doc-coauthoring/SKILL.md): fresh-reader testing is a useful example of validating whether an artifact answers its reader's questions.
- [OpenAI Notion research documentation](https://github.com/openai/plugins/blob/main/plugins/notion/skills/notion-research-documentation/SKILL.md): source IDs, gaps, citations and routing to focused references illustrate that evidence synthesis is already established practice.
